# TamLezzet ERP — Product Overview

> **The Complete Business Management Solution for Food Export Companies**

---

## What is TamLezzet ERP?

TamLezzet ERP is a modern, cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning system purpose-built for **food export businesses**. It brings together every aspect of your operations — from sourcing raw materials from farmers to delivering shipments to international buyers — into one unified platform accessible from any device, anywhere in the world.

No more scattered spreadsheets. No more missed follow-ups. No more guessing your cash flow. Everything your business needs, in one place.

---

## Who Is It For?

- Food export companies (spices, grains, pulses, dry fruits, processed foods)
- Agricultural trading businesses
- Import-export LLPs and Pvt. Ltd. companies
- Companies managing farmers, manufacturers, and international buyers simultaneously

---

## Modules & Features

### 1. Dashboard
Your business at a glance — the moment you log in.
- Total revenue, expenses, and profit for the current month
- Active shipments and their status
- Pending tasks and upcoming meetings
- Monthly trend charts for sales and expenses
- Expiring documents alerts
- Low stock warnings

---

### 2. Farmer Management
Manage your entire farmer network in one place.
- Complete farmer profiles — name, village, taluka, district, state, mobile
- Crop details — crop name, variety, harvest month and year
- Quality rating (1–5 stars) per farmer
- GPS location mapping of farms
- Photo gallery for each farmer
- Full purchase history — date, crop, quantity (kg), price per kg, total amount
- Payment status tracking — Pending / Paid / Partial
- Batch number tracking for traceability

---

### 3. Manufacturer Management
Keep track of all your processing and manufacturing partners.
- Company profiles with contact details, GST number, city, state
- Price per kg and minimum order quantity (MOQ)
- Lead time in days
- Quality score rating
- Certifications list (FSSAI, ISO, Organic, etc.)
- Agreement document storage
- Active/inactive status management

---

### 4. Inventory Management
Real-time stock visibility across your warehouse.
- Item master — name, type (Raw Material / Finished Goods / Packaging / Semi-Finished), SKU, batch number
- Current stock quantity with unit (KG, GRAM, PIECES, BOX)
- Reorder level alerts
- Cost per unit tracking
- Expiry date and manufacture date tracking
- Stock movements — Inward, Outward, Adjustment, Transfer
- Full movement history with reference numbers

---

### 5. Sales & Invoicing
Professional invoicing for your international buyers.
- Sales invoice creation with multi-currency support (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, etc.)
- Automatic INR conversion with exchange rate
- Due date tracking and overdue alerts
- Payment receipt recording
- Outstanding amount calculation
- Invoice status — Unpaid / Partial / Paid / Overdue / Cancelled
- Invoice document upload and storage

---

### 6. Shipment Tracking
End-to-end export shipment management.
- Shipment number, buyer name, destination country and port
- Container number, vessel name, voyage number
- ETD (Estimated Time of Departure) and ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival)
- Actual arrival tracking
- Invoice number and shipping bill number
- Net weight, gross weight, invoice value (USD), freight cost
- Shipment status — Draft / Booked / Loading / Shipped / In Transit / Arrived / Delivered / Cancelled
- Payment status — Pending / Partial / Received / Overdue
- Document attachments (BL, packing list, certificate of origin, etc.)

---

### 7. CRM — Customer Management
Build and manage your international buyer relationships.
- Customer profiles — company name, contact person, email, WhatsApp, phone, country, city
- Customer stage pipeline — Lead → Prospect → Sample Sent → Negotiation → Buyer → Inactive
- Interested products list per customer
- Notes and follow-up history
- Sample request management — product, specification, dispatch date, tracking number, feedback
- Sample status — Requested / Dispatched / Delivered / Approved / Rejected

---

### 8. Finance Dashboard
Complete financial visibility without an accountant.
- Profit & Loss summary
- Monthly cash flow chart (income vs expenses)
- Sales by country breakdown
- Top customers by revenue
- Revenue, expense, and profit trends over 12 months

---

### 9. Expense Management
Track every rupee spent in your business.
- Expense categories — Travel, Fuel, Hotel, Packaging, Freight, Manufacturer, Farmer, Office, Marketing, Salary, CA, Bank Charges, Miscellaneous
- Payment modes — Cash, Bank Transfer, UPI, Cheque, Card, Credit
- GST amount tracking
- Invoice/receipt upload
- Approval workflow — Pending / Approved / Rejected
- Fiscal year tagging
- Summary reports by category and date range

---

### 10. Document Management
All your business documents in one secure place.
- Document categories — LLP, GST, FSSAI, IEC, Trademark, Contract, Invoice, Lab Report, Bank, Insurance, Other
- Issue date and expiry date tracking
- Expiry alerts on dashboard
- Reference number (registration number, licence number)
- Secure cloud storage (AWS S3)
- Search and filter by category

---

### 11. Meeting Management
Never miss a business meeting or follow-up.
- Schedule meetings with title, date/time, duration, location or video link
- Agenda and minutes recording
- Follow-up action items
- Attendees list
- Meeting status — Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled / Postponed
- Attachment uploads
- Voice note URL support

---

### 12. Task Management
Keep your team on track with clear assignments.
- Task title, description, assigned to, assigned by
- Priority levels — Low / Medium / High / Critical
- Status tracking — To Do / In Progress / Review / Done / Cancelled
- Due date and completion date
- Notes and attachments
- Filter tasks by assignee

---

### 13. User Management & Security
Enterprise-grade security for your business data.
- Role-based access control — Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Accountant, Staff, Viewer
- Each role has specific permissions per module
- JWT-based authentication with auto token refresh
- Two-Factor Authentication (2FA/MFA) via TOTP (Google Authenticator)
- Session management with refresh tokens
- Audit trail — every record shows who created it and when

---

### 14. AI Assistant *(Premium Add-on)*
Ask questions about your business in plain English.
- "What is my total revenue this month?"
- "Which farmer supplied the most in 2024?"
- "Show me all overdue invoices"
- Powered by OpenAI GPT-4
